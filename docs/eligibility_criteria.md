# Study eligibility criteria

## Inclusion criteria

1. Original experimental studies involving Bacillus strains.
2. Quantitative measurements of biofilm formation or metal biosorption.
3. Identification of the bacterial strain.
4. Description of the experimental conditions.
5. Availability of numerical results or extractable quantitative data.

## Exclusion criteria

1. Reviews without original experimental data.
2. Studies reporting only antimicrobial or metal resistance.
3. Studies without identifiable bacterial strains.
4. Qualitative observations without measurable phenotypes.
5. Studies lacking sufficient information to associate the phenotype with a specific strain.

## Genomic correspondence

Genome availability will be evaluated separately.

Only genomes corresponding to the experimentally evaluated strain will be accepted for genotype–phenotype analyses.

Species-level substitutions will not be permitted.
