# Literature search protocol

## Research objective

Identify experimental studies reporting quantitative
biofilm formation or metal biosorption phenotypes
in identifiable Bacillus strains.

## Literature databases

- PubMed
- Scopus
- Web of Science

## Search strategy

Two independent searches will be conducted:

1. Bacillus AND biofilm formation
2. Bacillus AND metal biosorption

## Data extraction

For each eligible study, record:

- Publication identifier and DOI
- Bacillus species and strain
- Experimentally measured phenotype
- Metal evaluated, when applicable
- Experimental conditions
- Quantitative measurements and units
- Availability of a matching genome

## Reproducibility

Exact search strings, database-specific syntax,
search dates and retrieval counts must be recorded
before screening.

## Status

Protocol draft. Search strings and screening
procedures pending finalization.
